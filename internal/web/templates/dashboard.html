New to billing? How it fits together
  1. PlanWhat you sell and its price, e.g. “Pro, 29,000 a month”.
  2. CustomerA person or business who pays you.
  3. SubscriptionA customer signed up to a plan. It renews by itself each period.
  4. InvoiceThe bill. One is created when they subscribe and before every renewal.
  5. PaymentMoney received for an invoice. Once paid, the customer has access until the next renewal.

If a renewal isn't paid, the customer keeps access for a few grace days, then the subscription ends. You can change those days in Settings.
